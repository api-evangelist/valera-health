## What an agent can and cannot do here

Valera Health is a care-delivery organization, not a software vendor. There is **no
public API, no developer program, no SDK, no webhook surface and no machine-readable
contract** of any kind. Nothing on this page can be called programmatically.

- No OpenAPI, Swagger, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL or Postman collection is published.
- No `/.well-known/` document is served on any Valera Health host (12 paths probed
  across two hosts, all 404).
- No MCP server and no A2A agent card exist.
- `api.valerahealth.com` resolves (AWS ALB, Node/Express) but is the private backend for
  the Valera Health patient application. It answers 404 for every documented and
  undocumented discovery path and is not an integration surface.
- The clinical record and patient portal are operated by **NextGen Healthcare** — the
  site's own "Patient Portal" link targets a tenant path on `pxpportal.nextgen.com`,
  which returned 404 to an unauthenticated probe on 2026-09-02. Any FHIR or HL7 surface
  serving these patients belongs to that vendor, not to Valera Health.

An agent asked to integrate with Valera Health should route the request to a human: the
correct next step is the contact form, not a credential request.

## Services

- Individual therapy and psychiatry, delivered virtually by employed clinicians
- Group therapy
- Comprehensive Dialectical Behavior Therapy (DBT)
- Suicide care
- Youth Support Team (YST) programs for children and adolescents
- Care-coordinator model: intake form, scheduling, then a dedicated care team
